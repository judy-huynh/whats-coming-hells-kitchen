# What's Coming to Hell's Kitchen

A map of what is changing in Hell's Kitchen, Manhattan: storefronts coming soon and just opened, vacant storefronts, new buildings and demolitions, zoning proposals, denied applications, and who was in each building before.

Built from NYC and New York State open data: Buildings Dept. permits, State Liquor Authority applications, Health Dept. restaurant inspections, the storefront registry, city business licenses, Board of Standards and Appeals decisions, City Planning zoning and tax lot data, and MTA ridership.

Some labels are best guesses from public records, and every card links back to its source record. Data as of Nov 22, 2026.

By Judy Huynh, [judyhuynh.ca](https://judyhuynh.ca)
